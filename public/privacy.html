Privacy Policy

Variant Cards (“the App”) shows each product variant as its own product card on Shopify stores’ collection and search pages. This policy explains what information the App uses. Last updated: 2026-09-30.

Information the App accesses

  • Products and collections (titles, options, variants, images, prices, collection names) — to show each variant as its own card and to let you order or hide cards in a collection.
  • Theme configuration (read-only) — to tell you whether the App's theme embed is turned on.

The App does not access customer, order or payment data, and it does not use cookies or tracking on your storefront.

Where information is stored

Your settings (shop-wide and per collection) are saved inside your own Shopify store as app-owned metafields. The App has no database and does not copy your store data to its own servers. The storefront scripts run from Shopify's CDN and read product data from your store's own storefront.

Support chat

The App's pages in your Shopify admin include a live support chat provided by Tidio. It loads when you open the App. If you chat with us, Tidio processes your messages, your store's domain and your admin language so that we can reply; see Tidio's privacy policy. The chat is only in the App's admin pages, never on your storefront. Ask us in the chat or by email to delete a conversation.

Webhooks and requests

Shopify sends the App mandatory privacy webhooks (customer data requests, customer redaction, shop redaction). Because the App stores no personal data, it only verifies and acknowledges them. The hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

Uninstalling

When you uninstall the App, Shopify deletes the App's data from your store automatically after a retention period. The App never changes or deletes your products.

Your rights

You can view, change or delete the App's data at any time from the App. If you are located in the EEA, the UK or California, you have rights to access, correct and delete personal information; the App stores no personal information about your customers, and support chat conversations can be deleted on request (see Support chat).
