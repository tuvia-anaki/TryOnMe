Privacy Policy

Variant Cards (“the App”) shows each product variant as its own product card on Shopify stores’ collection and search pages. This policy explains what information the App uses. Last updated: 2026-09-30.

Information the App accesses

  • Product data (titles, options, variants, product images and their alt text) — to let you assign images to variants and to set each variant's image when you ask it to.
  • Theme configuration (read-only) — to tell you whether the App's theme embed is turned on.

The App does not access customer, order or payment data, and it does not use cookies or tracking on your storefront.

Where information is stored

Your settings (shop-wide and per collection) are saved inside your own Shopify store as app-owned metafields. The App has no database and does not copy your store data to its own servers. The storefront scripts run from Shopify's CDN and read product data from your store's own storefront.

Automatic matching by image colors runs in your browser; images are not uploaded anywhere.

Webhooks and requests

Shopify sends the App mandatory privacy webhooks (customer data requests, customer redaction, shop redaction). Because the App stores no personal data, it only verifies and acknowledges them. The hosting provider (Cloudflare) may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

Uninstalling

When you uninstall the App, Shopify deletes the App's data from your store automatically after a retention period. You can remove it immediately from the App's Settings → Remove all data before uninstalling. Your product images are never modified or deleted by the App.

Your rights

You can view, change or delete the App's data at any time from the App. If you are located in the EEA, the UK or California, you have rights to access, correct and delete personal information; since the App does not store personal information about you or your customers, such requests are satisfied by the controls above.
